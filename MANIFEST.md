# Manifest: Dynamic Health Data Routing & Ingestion Layer

## 1. Vision & Purpose
This repository provides a high-performance, decoupled data routing and translation engine designed to bridge legacy health information systems with modern interoperability standards (HL7 FHIR R4, FSE 2.0, USCDI).

The core objective is to eliminate onboarding bottlenecks for healthcare providers, enable resilient cross-border data exchange, and empower active citizen-driven health data management.

---

## 2. Core Architectural Pillars

* **Decoupled Transport:** Separates data ingestion from underlying storage/persistence logic, ensuring zero-lock-in and low-latency transformation.
* **Multi-Standard Translation:** Native mapping capabilities between national profiles (e.g., Italian FSE 2.0 / `it-fse-support`), cloud health ecosystems (Google Cloud Healthcare API, Apple HealthKit), and international emergency standards.
* **Agnostic & Resilient:** Designed to operate independently of single-jurisdiction databases, ensuring business continuity in hostile network environments or emergency scenarios.

---

## 3. Key Use Cases

### A. Emergency & Cross-Border Health Data Routing
* **Scenario:** Rapid access to vital records (e.g., blood group, severe drug allergies like NSAIDs/Diclofenac) during emergency transport or international travel.
* **Mechanism:** Instant transformation of payload into regional/national emergency schemas without requiring full prior onboarding of foreign medical teams.

### B. Citizen-Centric Active Ingestion (Patient-Led Record Population)
* **Scenario:** Overcoming "data siloing" in private specialist clinics (e.g., allergists, physiotherapists, dentists) or unintegrated regional facilities.
* **Mechanism:** Citizens can securely upload or authorize structured records via encrypted endpoints, bypassing legacy IT barriers to ensure critical data reaches first responders and primary care physicians.

### C. Continuous Telemetry & Wearable Integration (Sports & Prevention)
* **Scenario:** Ingestion of daily health metrics (heart rate variability, oxygenation, activity logs) generated by consumer devices (smartwatches, IoT fitness trackers).
* **Mechanism:** Maps vendor-agnostic metrics (LOINC / SNOMED-CT) into FHIR `Observation` profiles, making personal health data actionable for General Practitioners (GPs) and preventative care programs.
